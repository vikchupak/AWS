# Amazon QuickSight

Amazon QuickSight is a fully managed AWS business intelligence (BI) service used to analyze data and create interactive dashboards, visualizations, and reports.

It lets you connect to data sources, analyze the data, and create interactive dashboards, charts, and reports.
